Fixes cut marks disappearing on printed business-card/label sheets because the driver clipping area was set to the card-layout margins. Requests the printer's largest supported printable area and validates the actual selected printer area against artwork and external cut-mark extents before sending a job. Preserves physical card dimensions and sheet packing; incompatible paper/printable margins produce an explicit correction message instead of silently clipping cut marks. Includes page selection and single-sided defaults from 0.1.36. Physical printer verification remains required.
