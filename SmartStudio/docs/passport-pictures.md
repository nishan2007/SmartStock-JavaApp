# Passport Pictures

For multiple people, use the [shared Photo Queue](photo-queue.md) to upload photos
before opening a template and select them from any paired store computer.

Choose **Passport Pictures** in SmartStudio's workspace chooser. Load a JPEG or
PNG, choose a template, and use **Frame photo** to crop, zoom, and position it.
The sheet preview shows the same arrangement used for printing.

- Guyana: four photos, each 35 mm wide × 45 mm tall, in a 2 × 2 layout.
- US: two photos, each exactly 2 × 2 inches, stacked vertically.

Both templates use portrait 4 × 6 inch photo paper with space between photos.
Printing defaults to 4 × 6 and preserves physical dimensions; incompatible paper
or printer margins cause an error instead of shrinking or clipping the photos.
Confirm the photo tray and photo paper type in the printer's settings.

Guyana guidance permits photos between 26 × 32 mm and 35 × 45 mm:
https://guyanahctrinidad.mission.gov.gy/passports/
US guidance specifies 2 × 2 inches:
https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/passports/how-apply/photos.html

Framing does not validate passport eligibility, face dimensions, or background.
Check the applicable authority's requirements. Verify a physical print with a
ruler before production use; printer driver and paper behavior require a live test.
