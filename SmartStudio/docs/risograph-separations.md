# Risograph paper masters

Open Business Cards or Labels and choose **Riso separations…**. Select the source pages; separation output is always single-sided. The original template and editable objects remain unchanged. Ink settings apply to this job only.

Choose 1–8 suggested inks, then click **Detect inks**. Suggestions use the chosen output sheets, including merged data. Rename inks, change their colour, disable them, or remove shades to merge them into the remaining palette. White is paper. Photos and gradients are approximated using the closest selected ink with grayscale coverage, without colour calibration or simulated ink overprinting.

Choose a sheet and **Combined preview** or an individual ink, then **Update preview**. Optional registration crosses have identical positions on all plates; overlapping artwork/cut marks and printer clipping are rejected. Leave enough margin for the crosses.

Use **Picture inks…** to override automatic separation for individual image objects. Select an ink in the picture's **Print using** cell, or Ctrl/Shift-select several rows and use **Assign ink**. A thumbnail identifies the selected picture. Assigned artwork uses its grayscale tones on that ink only, preserving transparency, crop, rotation, layering and repeated sheet placements. Other objects use the chosen automatic palette. Objects sharing the same source image can have different assignments. Choose **Automatic separation** to remove an override. Assignments apply only to the current job and are included in preview, direct printing and PDF output; they do not recolour the saved template.

Renaming or recolouring an ink retains its picture assignments. An assigned ink cannot be removed until its pictures are reassigned; disabling an assigned ink blocks output with a correction message. Detecting a new palette requires confirmation before resetting assignments. Only image objects have manual ink overrides; editable text, barcodes and shapes continue to use palette separation.

**Export enabled inks PDF** creates lossless 300 dpi grayscale pages at the configured physical paper size, grouped by ink then sheet. PDF page labels identify the ink and sheet without adding lettering to the master. **Print selected ink…** prints all selected sheets for the enabled ink shown in the preview list, at actual size, single-sided. Choose the matching paper in the printer dialog. Print each separation on a normal printer, scan it on the Risograph, and use the corresponding ink drum.

Verify paper dimensions, registration, and photo tones on the physical equipment before production. The combined preview is approximate. Screen-print halftones, white underbases, and calibrated multi-ink photographic separations are not included in this Risograph workflow.
