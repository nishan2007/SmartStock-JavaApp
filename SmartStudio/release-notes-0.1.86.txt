SmartStudio 0.1.86

Keeps printing inside SmartStudio. Printer hardware margins are now queried only after the user selects a printer, preventing the default printer's margins from being carried to another device. Preserves the selected driver's accepted area and explicitly requests the sheet orientation.

Includes original-resolution/vector PDF export improvements.
Print Setup includes Copy print details for collecting local, geometry-only diagnostics after a direct print.

One-sheet direct printing passed on the Canon iR2525 with Evergreen's 12-card Letter layout. Physical C5240 acceptance is pending. Test one sheet before printing a full job.
