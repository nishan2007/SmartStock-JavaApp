Adds Photo Queue for multiple people's passport pictures. Scan one phone upload link, add optional names, and upload repeatedly over Wi-Fi or mobile data without leaving a template open. Any authorized computer paired with the store can preview and select queued photos for Guyana or US passport printing. Staff confirm successful printing before the server starts the 24-hour deletion timer; reprints do not extend it. Also includes direct-to-template phone capture. Requires SmartStock store server 1.1.42 and the updated website. Physical phone and printer acceptance remain separate verification steps.

Also includes the barcode selection toolbar (value, format, caption visibility, size and rotation); picture-size toolbar for cards, labels and badge pictures (mm/inches, current dimensions, aspect ratio locked by default for typed and canvas resizing); clearer barcode caption controls; and Publisher cloud-callout recovery/Windows line-break fixes used for Farood labels. Existing Farood shared-template repairs are saved separately on the store server.
