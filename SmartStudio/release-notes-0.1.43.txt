Adds persistent linked Riso sub-pages to Business Cards and Labels. Create, rename, recolour and remove sub-pages; move groups of whole editable objects between them using selection controls or dragging object-list rows. Sub-pages store object IDs, keeping one source for all geometry, text, styling and images. Main-design edits update linked views; new objects remain Unassigned until organised or explicitly excluded. Removing a sub-page preserves its artwork. Includes local Undo/Redo, sidebar child previews, grayscale single-sided printing and lossless 300 dpi PDF output with the original sheet layout.

Apply organiser changes to the template, then save normally. Linked templates use publication format 2, requiring SmartStock server 1.1.45 for shared saves and SmartStudio 0.1.43 on editing clients. Older clients reject the new format to prevent silently discarding links. Existing format-1 templates remain supported. Whole objects are supported now; picture-area splitting is deferred.
