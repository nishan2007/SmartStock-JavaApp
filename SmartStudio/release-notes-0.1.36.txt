SmartStudio 0.1.36 includes the editor and printing improvements since 0.1.28:
- Letter paper defaults for cards, labels and badges.
- Working design undo/redo shortcuts and buttons.
- Scalable, scrollable page thumbnails for multipage templates.
- Resizable Details/Properties/Insert/Layers panel.
- Text formatting toolbar: font, size, bold, italic, underline, strike, colour and outline.
- Publisher image-layer imports retain the requested 300 DPI resolution.
- Automatic NFC badge listening on the login screen, with PIN-policy enforcement and held-card suppression.
- Select all, current or individual template pages to print. Every job defaults to single-sided; optional front/back pairing supports long- or short-edge flipping and previews the backs.
