SmartStudio 0.1.11 (Windows build 1011)

Fixes badge template dragging stopping after the first movement. Fields remain selected throughout a drag. Click a field on the canvas, then use arrow keys to move it 0.1 mm, or Shift + arrow for 1 mm.

Add rectangles, ellipses, triangles and lines to badge templates. Edit size and colour in the layer table; use Bring forward and Send backward to position shapes around text and photos. Shapes are saved in templates and appear in previews, image/PDF exports and printed cards. Includes SmartStock client 1.1.25.
