SmartStock 1.1.27 (Windows build 101027)

Custom orders now round the final order total once, after unit prices, quantities, add-ons and discounts are summed. A $30 item ordered 100 times totals $3,000; per-item rounding no longer accumulates. This applies to new orders, size sets, pending pricing, quotation conversion and shirt estimates. Existing finalized orders are not repriced.

Includes mixed-size custom-order sets, individual-item editing within sets, held-draft support, shirt catalogue setup and the website shirt designer with print-area editing and store quote review. Includes the current publishing workspace and grouped order slips.

Update the store server before registers. Website features require the updated store server. Validate physical printing and store workflows before production use.
