SmartStock 1.0.132

- Saves checkout timing diagnostics to the register's local .smartstock/checkout-timing.log, including server response, drawer command, receipt work, and total background checkout time.
- Timestamps each timing entry and rotates the log with one retained backup to bound disk usage.
- Keeps customer and payment details out of timing logs; logging failures never interrupt a sale.
- Includes the existing drawer-before-receipt checkout improvement and previous Wallet enhancements.
- Physical drawer response and live register timing verification remain required after updating.
