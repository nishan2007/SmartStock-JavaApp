SmartStock 1.0.126

- Adds a Use Last Saved Details button to the Add Item web app for quickly restoring the previous department, item type, brand, and sales shelf.
- Adds optional customer photos and business logos to customer account profiles.
- Stores customer photos in the protected OneDrive-backed customer-photos folder with a local image cache.
- Adds customer photo and expiry information to landscape customer-card output and template positioning.
- Defaults newly issued customer cards to a two-year expiry while allowing the expiry date to be changed before printing or PDF export.
- Adds a Renew Card action and preserves the saved expiry date for later reprints.
- Prevents expired customer cards from being used for account-credit purchases without blocking other payment methods, returns, payments, or account access.
- Adds the Government customer type and branded customer-card template while preserving existing customized templates.
- Includes the ordered database upgrade for customer photo references, card issue dates, expiry dates, and Government template mapping.
- Allows scheduler web sign-in with either a SmartStock username or email address.
- Adds server-enforced, read-only employee self-service schedule viewing.
- Shows the scheduler link and QR code on authorized registers while keeping start and stop controls server-only.
- Adds an employee picker before Auto Schedule, selecting all non-administrator employees by default.
