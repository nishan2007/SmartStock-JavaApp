SmartStock 1.0.139

- Fixes Apple Wallet enrollment links for the installed Windows background service by supporting signing material deployed in the protected server-owned SmartStock profile.
- Keeps successful signed-pass downloads available when the secondary issuance audit write fails.
- Adds sanitized machine-local Wallet enrollment failure diagnostics without recording enrollment tokens or certificate passwords.
- Preserves the isolated loopback-only Wallet gateway, one-time enrollment tokens, revocable credentials, barcode login, PIN policy, and disabled-by-default NFC behavior.
- Does not enable Apple Wallet NFC; a certified Apple VAS provider and reader are still required.
