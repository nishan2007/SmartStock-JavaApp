Quotation and invoice slips now show Original Total (quantity x unit price) before discount and final amount. The description column is wider for longer item names.

Subtotal and Grand Total are stacked at the bottom. Subtotal adds the final line amounts with cents; Grand Total rounds to the nearest whole currency unit for cash display. For example, subtotal 279,000.11 displays a grand total of 279,000. Stored invoice amounts, payments, and balances retain their existing precision.
