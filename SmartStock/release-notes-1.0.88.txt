Inventory cost-price preference:
- Adds a per-store Company Preference controlling whether Cost Price is required when adding an inventory item.
- Keeps Cost Price required by default so existing stores retain their current workflow.
- Saves a blank Cost Price as $0.00 when the preference is disabled.
- Applies the preference to both the desktop and mobile Add Item forms and enforces it on the store server.
- Safely upgrades existing local databases with the new preference column.
