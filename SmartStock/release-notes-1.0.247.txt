Fixes invoice payments appearing in customer history without reducing the recalculated account balance. Repairs affected existing invoice payment ledger entries when customer accounts are loaded, using matching payment allocations.

Standardizes customer phone numbers on save to +countrycode followed by digits. Seven-digit Guyana numbers receive +592; existing international country codes are preserved. Spaces, brackets, dashes and 00 international prefixes are normalized. Blank numbers remain optional and incomplete numbers show a validation message. The same rule applies to customer phone updates from custom orders.

Includes all current SmartStock application changes and the catalog recovery improvements from 1.0.246. Existing customer phone numbers are normalized when their accounts are next saved.
