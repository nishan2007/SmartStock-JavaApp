SmartStock 1.1.7

- Edit a selected custom-order cart line, restore its fields and attachments, and use Update Line to replace it. Cancel Edit preserves the original.
- Drag files from Explorer into the custom-order attachment window. Duplicate files are ignored and the 15-file limit remains.
- Later custom-order payments accept comma-formatted amounts and show request failures directly.
- Includes the latest application source and resources, including the Compact quotation/invoice layout and fixes from 1.1.6.
