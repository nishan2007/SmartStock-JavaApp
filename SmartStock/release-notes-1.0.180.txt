SmartStock 1.0.180

Improve barcode scanning reliability and item lookup speed at checkout.

- Prevent a repeated scan of the same barcode from cancelling and restarting the lookup already in progress.
- Stop the normal product-search debounce from competing with an Enter-terminated barcode scan.
- Reduce unnecessary database response work during exact barcode and SKU lookups.
- Restore focus to the checkout item field after a lookup failure so the next scan is received correctly.
- Include the employee application portal, customer charge authorization and automatic discounts, product variants and catalog fields, receipt and cash-drawer improvements, scheduler reliability, and updater fixes accumulated through 1.0.179.

Update the store server before registers so the matching schema and authenticated lookup behavior are available.
