SmartStock 1.0.207

Improve custom-item cataloging, sales, and price-tag workflows.

- Add optional size and color identifiers to custom items and variants.
- Add brand support for custom items and variants, including variant inheritance.
- Allow photos to be handed off from the desktop form and uploaded from a phone.
- Add an opt-in “Sell in normal POS” setting for each custom item.
- Sell enabled blank shirts, cups, and their variants through the normal checkout flow without creating a print order.
- Preserve custom item identity on receipts and returns and update the correct custom inventory record.
- Support custom parent items and variants in price-tag printing.
