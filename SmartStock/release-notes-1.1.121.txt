SmartStock 1.1.121 - preserve signed stock during inventory merges

Duplicate item merges now combine signed quantities, so negative stock is subtracted rather than blocked or discarded. For example, 56 and -11 consolidate to 45; -3 and 82 consolidate to 79. Each store applies only its own stock once, including when a merge is retried. Review shows the signed-stock calculation. Integer overflow and underflow remain blocked.

Original photos, barcode aliases and historical sale amounts remain preserved. No database migration or model changes are required. Store servers must run this version before applying merges involving negative stock.
