SmartStock 1.1.41

Fix incoming product synchronization between stores. Product creation and edit
notifications now apply the sending store's verified catalog snapshot, including
names, prices, colors, descriptions, variants, pictures, and additional barcodes.
Previously received notifications are retried automatically after the update.
Outgoing product notifications include the catalog version so receiving stores
can wait for the matching snapshot without copying quantity-only adjustments.

Store stock counts and transactions remain local. Newer local product edits are
preserved, conflicting catalog identities are reported, and failed deliveries
remain available for retry instead of being marked as applied.
