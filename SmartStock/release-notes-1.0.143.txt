SmartStock 1.0.143

- Adds Apple Wallet Lock Screen relevance for employee badge passes at configured store locations.
- Includes every coordinate-configured store assigned to the employee, up to Apple's 10-location pass limit.
- Adds Wallet latitude and longitude fields to Location Management with validation and cross-store synchronization.
- Prioritizes the issuing store and omits stores whose Wallet coordinates have not been configured.
- Existing Wallet passes must be replaced after store coordinates are saved to receive the new location information.
