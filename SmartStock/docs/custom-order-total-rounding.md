# Custom-order rounding

The custom-order rounding preference applies once to the final order total.
Each unit keeps its exact price after add-ons and discounts; quantities are
summed before rounding to the nearest $20. Halfway totals round upward.
With rounding disabled, the summed total is charged without the $20 adjustment.

Examples: $30 × 100 = $3,000; $30 × 3 = $90, rounded once to $100 if enabled.
Changing one item's price affects that unit before the total is calculated.
Minimum deposits, payment limits and balances use the final rounded order total.

This rule applies to new orders, mixed-size sets, pending-price finalization,
quotation conversion and website shirt estimates. Existing finalized orders are
not repriced. Stock remains based on the individual saved units.
