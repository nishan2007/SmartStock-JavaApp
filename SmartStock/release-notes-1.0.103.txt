SmartStock 1.0.103

- Adds automatic MIFARE Classic 1K support alongside NTAG213/215/216 and MIFARE Ultralight employee badges.
- Programs and reads the same employee Badge ID for login, time clock, badge testing, and manager authorization workflows.
- Stores Classic badge records only in sector 1 data blocks 4-6 and never writes the sector trailer containing keys and access controls.
- Authenticates Classic cards before access, validates stored records with an integrity checksum, and safely rejects locked or previously re-keyed cards.
- Improves badge programming results by reporting the detected NFC card family after successful write-and-read-back verification.
