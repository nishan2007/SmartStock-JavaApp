SmartStock 1.1.33

Daily-rate payroll now supports an explicit Half Day selection for each employee work date.
Double-click an employee's payroll summary and tick or clear Half Day in Work Sessions; pay recalculates to 50% or the full daily rate.
Daily-rate employees can mark their own completed day using the Half Day button after clock-out.
Multiple sessions on a date share one setting, and payroll periods with recorded payments are protected from classification changes.
Includes the LAN HTTPS transport fix that avoids the Windows reverse-DNS stall before TLS negotiation.
Application packages retain inference runtimes and licences and exclude AI model weights.
